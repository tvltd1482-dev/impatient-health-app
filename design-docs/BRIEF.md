## What iMpatient Is

iMpatient is a **behavioral intelligence engine** for complex human lives.

It is not a health journal. It is not a wellness app. It is not a chronic-illness tracker.

It is the **administrative intelligence layer** between a person's life, their body, their care system, and their geography — built so the user does not have to hold all of it in their head, in spreadsheets, or in their nervous system.

> *"Pattern recognition, forecasting, assessment, and engineered indexing — to increase the lived experience of our users."*
> — Azure Antoinette, Founder

---

## Who iMpatient Is For

**Everyone whose life and body are in conversation** — which is everyone, eventually.

The product is built from chronic-illness lived experience because that population needs it most acutely. But the engine reads patterns. Patterns are not illness — they are life. The product serves:

- **The Observer** — undiagnosed, newly diagnosed, or "the worried well." Knows something is happening; can't yet name it.
- **The Manager** — has a diagnosis, has the care team, learning to live alongside it.
- **The Complex Human** — multi-condition, high-functioning, exhausted. Stakes are highest.
- **The Partner** — clinicians and employers who carry responsibility for a population.

Same engine. Different language at the surface. Different depth of integration. Different price.

## The Six Core Surfaces

The product is not a dashboard with feature tiles. It is six surfaces, each answering a specific question the user has about their life.

### 1. Today — *"What is true right now?"*
Current read of body, calendar, money, location, and mental load. The lede is literary, not clinical. Persona-aware: a Free user sees their week-in-review; a Pro user sees the prediction window for the next 96 hours.

### 2. Foresight — *"What's coming, and what should I do now?"*
The trip, the appointment, the launch week, the school holiday. Long-range pattern projection. **Vacation is a clinical event** — a 7-day sensory-load forecast, not recovery. The engine knows which day will break, which mornings to protect, where to build a quiet hour into a theme park.

### 3. Admin — *"What does Coach owe my life this week?"*
The appointment queue. The refill queue. The insurance queue. The document queue. Coach handles, user approves. **Vagal calm is the deliverable** — that the user does not have to remember any of it.

### 4. Travel Card — *"My medical file, in my pocket, never in my luggage."*
Encrypted, agent-fronted credential. Conditions, meds, allergies, infusion schedule, MCAS triggers, emergency contacts, specialist protocols. Generated on demand. Shareable as one-page PDF or QR code at an ER intake. **The user does not travel with their full file. No one should.**

### 5. Patterns — *"What is the evidence?"*
Indexed correlation library. The user's own data, organized by the relationships the engine has found and the user has confirmed. This is what gets shared with a doctor. This is what wins an insurance appeal.

### 6. Cost of Care — *"What is my illness costing me?"*
Out-of-pocket spend tracked alongside flares. Per-trip, per-month, per-quarter, per-condition. Healthcare overhead made visible because **budgeting your illness is part of living with it**, especially when it will never be covered by insurance.

## Signal Classes (the Inputs Layer)

The engine reads from these signal classes. The user owns every byte. Tier determines depth, never ownership.

### Wearables / Biometrics
Apple Health, Apple Watch, Oura, Garmin, Withings, Whoop, Fitbit, Dexcom, Polar, Pulsetto, Nurosym. The constellation, not a single device.

### Calendars (plural, by domain)
Apple, Google, Outlook, iCloud — separated by life domain: **work, personal, family, board, care-team**. Most users live across at least two.

### Communications
Gmail, Outlook, iMessage / SMS volume, Slack (volume only, never content). Used for cognitive-load and after-hours patterns.

### Travel
TripIt, United, Lufthansa, Delta, Qantas, airline-specific apps. Flights, delays, gate changes. Climate + barometric pressure deltas at origin and destination (Tomorrow.io). Hotel check-in awareness.

### Location *(new — primary signal class)*
Continuous, user-controlled. The engine reads:
- **Inside a clinical setting** (hospital, doctor's office, ER, infusion center, lab) → biomarkers spike → protocol question: *whose appointment is this?*
- If the user's: pre-load context, queue post-visit summary, watch for vagal markers
- If a family member's or friend's: switch to **caregiver mode** — distress is empathic; surface caregiver-load metrics
- **Unfamiliar location + elevated biomarkers** → proactive: "You're at Children's Mercy ER. Want me to start a record for whoever you're with?"
- **Returning home** after clinical event → 72h recovery protocol

### Care System
Specialist EHR feeds (×N), prescription/pharmacy systems, lab portals, infusion centers. Service-animal vet portals where applicable.

### Conditions Library *(new — citable global database)*
Every condition, term, and definition Coach uses is grounded in a citable public source: NIH, NHS, Mayo, MedlinePlus, peer-reviewed sources. Not WebMD. Not Wikipedia. **Sources a user can show their doctor. Sources a doctor will respect.** This is also the foundation for the agent's reasoning — Coach grounds its language in cited terminology, never invents a definition.

### Provider Directory *(new — paid, geo-aware)*
Licensed integration: NPPES + state licensing boards (US), GP/specialist registries (UK, EU, AUS, etc.). When the user lands in Geneva, the engine knows the three MCAS-aware specialists within 4km, their hospital affiliations, languages spoken, after-hours protocol. When the user moves cities, it surfaces "do you want help finding equivalent specialists here?"

### Money
Out-of-pocket spend by category. Healthcare insurance interactions (denials, appeals, EOBs). Subscription costs. The cost of being ill.

## What the Engine Does

Four engineered capabilities, all in service of lived experience:

1. **Pattern Recognition** — finds correlations across signal classes that the user has felt but couldn't prove
2. **Forecasting** — projects forward based on indexed history of comparable weeks
3. **Assessment** — reads current physiological + psychological + administrative state against personal baselines (never population averages)
4. **Engineered Indexing** — every signal becomes addressable, correlatable, exportable

These are not separate features. They are one engine — Mønk™ — running continuously.

## Voice & Tone

Literary, not clinical. Specific, not generic. Gentle, not clinical-warm-fake.

**Yes:**
- *"Your watch has been keeping a quieter notebook for you."*
- *"You've been told it's stress for two years. You're not making this up. The data has a shape."*
- *"She did not need to be asked. She saw the same shape you did."*
- *"Thursday is asking for a decision."*

**No:**
- *"Your wellness journey starts here!"*
- *"AI-powered insights for optimal health."*
- Clinical jargon without translation.
- Emoji.

The voice is the founder's voice. It earns the user's trust because it speaks to them like a person who has lived this, not a product that has researched it.

## Mental Health Is Not Separate

The vagal load read is continuous. **Sensory-overload prediction is a first-class signal class** — separate from physical flare prediction.

> "Today is mentally a 7/10 day. Drop the 3pm ad-hoc Slack call. Pickup the kids in the car not on foot. Tonight is not the night to call your mother."

Anxiety, sensory load, executive function fatigue, and emotional regulation are all biometric-readable and calendar-correlated. The engine treats them as data, not as a separate "wellness" silo.

---

## Privacy & Sovereignty

Three principles, non-negotiable:

1. **The user owns every byte.** Always. Forever. Exportable in raw form on demand.
2. **The Travel Card and any data leaving the user's device** is encrypted, agent-fronted, time-bound, and the user holds the keys.
3. **Aggregate enterprise intelligence is anonymized at the source.** Employers and clinicians never see individual data without explicit, revocable consent.

Mønk™ is built on Claude (Anthropic) for alignment with these principles.

## Tier Philosophy

The tiers are a **humanity ladder, not a feature ladder.**

| Tier | Archetype | Promise |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | The Observer | A 7-day mirror. Your data, read back to you. |
| **Essential — $24.99/mo** | The Manager | One life domain fully connected. 30-day index. Pattern discovery. |
| **Pro — $49.99/mo** | The Complex Human | All domains. Agentic Coach. 48–96h predictions. Travel Card. Cost of Care. Unlimited history. |
| **Enterprise — Custom** | The Partner (clinician / employer) | Population intelligence. Aggregate, anonymized. Per-seat licensed. |

Free is forever. Essential opens one domain. Pro opens the whole life. Enterprise serves the people, not the company.
